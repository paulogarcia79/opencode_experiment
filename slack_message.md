Our current publishing strategy is fragmented, and honestly, generic platforms just don't reflect our identity. We are sharing highly detailed articles—ranging from procedural generation mechanics to technical fermentation logs—and the current reading environment feels completely out of place.

I'd love for us to build our own unified Blog + Newsletter platform. The goal is to have total control over the reading experience and subscriber retention.

Here are my core ideas to get us started:

Accessibility from day zero: This is non-negotiable. For UI states (subscription errors, success messages, etc.), we cannot rely on color alone. I want us to apply triple coding (color, icon, and pattern) so it's 100% color-blind friendly.

Writing Experience: I need a completely distraction-free WYSIWYG editor in the admin panel. Just the strictly essential formatting tools.

Newsletter Capture: Something very subtle but effective at the end of the articles to capture emails. Absolutely no invasive pop-ups.

Can someone start scoping out the PRD (Product Requirements Document) for this? I want us to clearly define the specs before we even touch the database or the code.
