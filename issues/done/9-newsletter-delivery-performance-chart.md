## What to build

Visualize the health of the newsletter delivery system. This chart will show the ratio of successful to failed sends for recent newsletter blasts.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Chart showing delivery success vs. failure rates.
- [ ] Data is pulled from the `NewsletterSend` status fields.
- [ ] Provides clear feedback if there are recurring delivery failures (e.g., domain verification issues).
